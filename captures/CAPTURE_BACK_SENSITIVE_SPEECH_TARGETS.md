# Capture Back: Sensitive Speech Targets

## Status

Acknowledged by the human custodian on 2026-07-30.

## Proposal

Make every visible component of a generated flashcard speak its own Italian
target:

- subject image: the subject;
- verb image or icon: the conjugated form;
- complement image or icon: the exact complement phrase;
- bordered sentence control: the complete assembled sentence.

The change adds visible speaker, hover, focus, and pressed affordances and uses
native buttons for keyboard and assistive-technology access.

## Interpretation

The card is a composed language-use structure, not one undifferentiated image.
Independent speech lets the learner hear its parts and then the complete
utterance in the same order in which meaning is assembled.

## Boundary

This Capture Back does not change verb semantics, paradigms, complement pools,
or visual assets. In particular, the separately identified `provare` corpus
correction remains outside this interaction-only proposal.

This proposal does not acknowledge itself, commit, rebuild the pack, publish,
or push.
