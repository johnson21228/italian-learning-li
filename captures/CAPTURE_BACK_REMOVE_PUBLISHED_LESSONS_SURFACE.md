# Capture Back: Remove Published Lessons Surface

## Status

Acknowledged by the human custodian on 2026-07-30.

## Proposal

Remove from the published site:

- the Lessons navigation button;
- the lesson index and dated lesson page;
- the lesson-only stylesheet;
- published copies under `site/images/class-lessons/`.

The home page becomes a focused generative-flashcard surface.

## Preservation

This removes a publication projection, not the underlying knowledge. The
Markdown lesson, Markdown flashcards, privacy-safe source crops, Capture Backs,
and Git history remain in Workbench custody.

The reusable weekly protocol now captures new class material into the Workbench
without automatically creating a separate public Lessons area. Selected
material can still enter generated flashcards through a reviewed Capture Back.

## Boundary

This proposal does not acknowledge itself, commit, rebuild the pack, publish,
or push.
