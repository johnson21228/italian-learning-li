# Capture Back: Handout Flashcard Candidates

## Status

Acknowledged by the human custodian on 2026-07-30.

## Proposal

Use three external handout PDFs to create a broad lexical candidate registry,
eighteen concise learner-practice candidates, and a second site view titled
`From Handouts`.

The PDFs are not copied into Git, packs, or the published site. The Workbench
stores only:

- exact external-file hashes and rights notes;
- short or independently transformed practice phrases;
- source title, PDF page, part of speech, status, and derivation mode;
- a first expansion of 112 high-value lexical candidates;
- a site projection that keeps handout material separate from class material.

## Sources

- `Piacere!`: six beginner-conversation candidates;
- `Cortina Conversational Italian in 20 Lessons`: six transformed first-lesson candidates;
- `Basic Italian for Travelers`: six concise survival-language candidates.

## Copyright boundary

No page image, chapter, extended passage, phonetic table, or complete PDF is
stored or published. All-rights-reserved sources remain external reference
inputs. `Piacere!` attribution and CC BY-NC-SA status are recorded.

## Boundary

The lexical layer is a broad high-value extraction, not an exhaustive claim
over all 776 pages. These are proposed candidates, not classroom evidence and not a claim
that every sentence was spoken in class. This proposal does not acknowledge
itself, commit, rebuild the pack, publish, or push.
