# Capture Back: Generative Flashcard System

## Status

Acknowledged by the human custodian on 2026-07-30.

## Proposal

Replace the accumulated site card catalog with a generated practice projection
governed by `li/domain/generative_flashcard_system_rule.md`.

All eighteen verbs already represented in the Workbench are admitted:
`essere`, `stare`, `chiamarsi`, `andare`, `capire`, `ripetere`, `dire`,
`guardare`, `ascoltare`, `parlare`, `scrivere`, `abitare`, `provare`,
`mangiare`, `lavorare`, `venire`, `avere`, and `bere`.

Each produces six cards with every conjugation once and a distinct compatible
class-derived complement on every card, for 108 cards in each generated round.
The five previously reconciled verbs retain their image assets. The other
thirteen paradigms and sentence pairings are explicitly marked as
standard-language reconstruction grounded in the Workbench's learned-verb and
class-vocabulary corpus, not as complete tables visibly captured in class.

## Removed site surfaces

The proposal removes category filters, Supabase sign-in/upload controls, and
image-prompt buttons. Those capabilities are not part of the focused generated
practice loop.

## Preservation

The previous runtime catalog is removed from the site projection, not erased
from Workbench history. Git, LI, class assets, and acknowledged Capture Backs
retain its evidence and evolution.

## Boundary

This proposal does not acknowledge itself, commit, publish, rebuild the pack,
or push.
